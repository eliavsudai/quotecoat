Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account or cloud. To move to a new phone, use Settings → Export backup, then Settings → Import backup on the new phone.

How are prices calculated?

From your own rates in Settings → My rates: labor rate, how fast you paint each surface, paint coverage, your three paint prices, markup, supplies, tax and minimum job price. Quotes are estimates. Tap the totals bar in a job to see every number.

The text message has no PDF attached

Some carriers or settings don't allow attachments from apps. Use Email or More (share sheet) instead, or send through iMessage.

I'm not getting follow-up reminders

Allow notifications for QuoteCoat in iOS Settings → Notifications. Reminders are set when a quote is marked Sent.

Can I use meters and liters?

Yes. Settings → Units → Meters & liters. Your rates are converted automatically.

Does it cost anything after I buy it?

No. QuoteCoat is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no ads and no account.
