Privacy Policy

QuoteCoat · Last updated October 26, 2026

Short version: QuoteCoat does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your company profile, logo, rates, clients, jobs, rooms, quotes, signatures and job photos are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account, no login, no server and no cloud sync run by us.

What we collect

Nothing. QuoteCoat has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that send data anywhere. We never see your quotes, your customers or your photos.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. If you text, email or share a quote PDF, follow-up message or backup file, it goes through Apple's Messages, Mail or share sheet to the people and apps you pick. Quotes and photos never leave the phone unless you send them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of the job for your quotes.
  • Photos: to add your logo and job photos from your library.
  • Notifications: local reminders to follow up on quotes. They are created on your phone; no push service is used.

Each permission is asked only when you first use that feature, and you can change it anytime in iOS Settings.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all data in the app, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

QuoteCoat is a business tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
